# AI Transformation Playbook
A practical, PM-focused playbook for planning and delivering enterprise AI programs.

## Overview
This repository is designed for AI Transformation Program Managers, Product Leaders, and Enterprise Architects who need clear, structured, repeatable guidance for planning, governing, and scaling AI initiatives. It reflects how I lead AI programs: with clarity, alignment, measurable outcomes, and responsible governance.

## 📂 What’s Inside
This playbook includes the core artifacts required to run an enterprise AI program with discipline and clarity:
- **AI roadmap template** — strategic phases, milestones, and decision gates
- **Stakeholder map** — roles, responsibilities, and cross-functional alignment
- **Change management plan** — communication, training, and adoption strategy
- **Adoption KPIs** — measurable indicators of AI value and usage
- **Governance checklist** — responsible AI, risk, compliance, and quality controls
- **RACI Matrix** — accountability structure for AI delivery

## 🎯 Why This Matters
AI transformation succeeds when program structure, governance, and adoption are intentional. This repository demonstrates how I lead AI programs with:
- Clear strategy and measurable outcomes
- Strong cross-functional alignment
- Responsible AI governance
- Repeatable delivery frameworks
- Scalable adoption patterns
  
It serves as a practical reference for organizations modernizing processes, deploying AI responsibly, and building long-term capability.
