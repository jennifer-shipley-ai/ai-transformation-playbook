# Stakeholder Map

A structured view of stakeholders involved in AI transformation programs.

---

## Executive Stakeholders
- CIO / CTO
- Chief Data Officer
- Chief Digital Officer
- Business Unit VP(s)
- AI Governance Council

Role:
- Strategic alignment
- Funding
- Risk oversight

---

## Technical Stakeholders
- Data Engineering
- ML Engineering
- Architecture
- Security & Compliance
- DevOps

Role:
- Build, integrate, secure, and maintain AI solutions

---

## Business Stakeholders
- Product Owners
- Operations Leaders
- Process Owners
- End Users

Role:
- Requirements
- Adoption
- Feedback loop

---

## Governance Stakeholders
- Legal
- Risk Management
- Privacy Office
- Responsible AI Committee

Role:
- Policy enforcement
- Ethical review
- Compliance

---

## Change Management Stakeholders
- Training & Enablement
- Communications
- HR (for workforce impact)

Role:
- Adoption
- Upskilling
- Communication

---

## Stakeholder Influence Matrix

| Stakeholder Group | Influence | Interest | Engagement Strategy |
|-------------------|-----------|----------|---------------------|
| Executives | High | High | Steering committee, monthly updates |
| Technical Teams | High | Medium | Weekly syncs, architecture reviews |
| Business Teams | Medium | High | Workshops, demos, feedback loops |
| Governance | High | High | Policy reviews, risk checkpoints |
| End Users | Low | High | Training, pilot feedback |

---

This map ensures alignment, clarity, and proactive engagement across all groups.
