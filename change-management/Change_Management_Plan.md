# AI Change Management Plan

A structured plan to drive adoption and minimize resistance during AI transformation.

---

## 1. Change Vision
- Why AI?
- What outcomes will improve?
- How will work change?

---

## 2. Impact Assessment
- Roles affected
- Processes impacted
- Required skill changes
- Risk areas

---

## 3. Communication Strategy
- Executive messaging
- Business unit messaging
- User-level messaging
- Cadence & channels

---

## 4. Training & Enablement
- Role-based training
- Hands-on workshops
- Job aids & documentation
- AI literacy programs

---

## 5. Adoption Plan
- Pilot groups
- Feedback loops
- Iteration cycles
- Full rollout plan

---

## 6. Resistance Management
- Identify resistance points
- Mitigation strategies
- Leadership alignment
- Support channels

---

## 7. Success Metrics
- Adoption rate
- User satisfaction
- Productivity improvements
- Reduction in manual effort

---

## 8. Sustainment Plan
- Continuous training
- Governance checkpoints
- Performance monitoring
- Optimization cycles

---

This plan ensures AI adoption is intentional, supported, and measurable.
