# AI Governance Checklist

A practical checklist for ensuring responsible and compliant AI delivery.

---

## Strategy & Alignment
- Use case aligned to business goals
- Executive sponsor identified
- Success criteria defined

---

## Data Governance
- Data quality validated
- Privacy requirements met
- Sensitive data minimized
- Access controls implemented

---

## Model Governance
- Model documentation complete
- Bias testing performed
- Explainability reviewed
- Performance validated

---

## Risk & Compliance
- Legal review completed
- Risk assessment documented
- Regulatory requirements met
- Mitigation plan approved

---

## Security
- Threat modeling completed
- Secure model hosting
- API security validated
- Monitoring enabled

---

## Responsible AI
- Fairness considerations documented
- Human oversight defined
- Transparency ensured
- Lifecycle governance in place

---

## Deployment & Monitoring
- Rollout plan approved
- Monitoring dashboards active
- Drift detection configured
- Retraining plan defined

---

This checklist ensures AI solutions are safe, compliant, and trustworthy.
