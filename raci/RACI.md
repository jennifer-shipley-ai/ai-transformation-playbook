# RACI Matrix for AI Transformation

Defines roles and responsibilities across the AI program lifecycle.

---

## RACI Table

| Activity | Responsible (R) | Accountable (A) | Consulted (C) | Informed (I) |
|----------|------------------|------------------|----------------|--------------|
| Use Case Prioritization | Product Owner | Executive Sponsor | PM, Data Lead | Stakeholders |
| Requirements Gathering | PM | Product Owner | SMEs | Engineering |
| Architecture Design | Architect | CTO | PM, Security | Governance |
| Model Development | ML Engineer | Data Science Lead | PM | Stakeholders |
| Integration | Engineering | Architect | PM | Business Teams |
| Testing | QA | PM | Product Owner | Stakeholders |
| Deployment | DevOps | CTO | PM, Security | All Users |
| Monitoring | Data Science | PM | Governance | Executives |
| Retraining | ML Engineer | Data Science Lead | PM | Governance |

---

## Notes
- R = Does the work  
- A = Owns the outcome  
- C = Provides input  
- I = Needs awareness  

---

This RACI ensures clarity, accountability, and smooth cross-functional delivery.
