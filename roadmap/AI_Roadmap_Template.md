# AI Transformation Roadmap Template

A structured roadmap for planning and delivering enterprise AI initiatives. This template is designed for PMs, program leaders, and transformation teams driving AI adoption across business units.

---

## 1. Vision & Strategic Alignment
- Business outcomes targeted
- AI opportunities aligned to strategy
- Executive sponsors and steering committee
- Success definition (quantitative + qualitative)

---

## 2. Current State Assessment
- Existing data landscape
- Technical readiness
- Process maturity
- Workforce readiness
- Risks and constraints

---

## 3. AI Use Case Prioritization
Criteria:
- Business value
- Feasibility
- Data availability
- Risk level
- Time-to-impact

Deliverables:
- Prioritized use case list
- Use case scoring matrix

---

## 4. Architecture & Technical Foundations
- Data sources
- Integration points
- Model hosting strategy
- Security & compliance requirements
- Governance checkpoints

---

## 5. Delivery Phases
### Phase 1 — Discovery
- Requirements
- Stakeholder interviews
- Data profiling

### Phase 2 — Design
- Solution architecture
- Governance plan
- Change management plan

### Phase 3 — Build
- Model development
- Integration
- Testing

### Phase 4 — Deploy
- Release plan
- Monitoring setup
- User enablement

### Phase 5 — Scale
- Additional use cases
- Optimization
- Continuous improvement

---

## 6. KPIs & Measurement
- Adoption KPIs
- Operational KPIs
- Model performance KPIs
- Business impact KPIs

---

## 7. Risks & Mitigations
- Technical risks
- Data risks
- Compliance risks
- Change management risks

---

## 8. Communication Plan
- Cadence
- Channels
- Stakeholder groups
- Reporting templates

---

## 9. Responsible AI Considerations
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Privacy
- Human oversight
- Lifecycle governance

---

## 10. Roadmap Timeline (Example)
| Quarter | Milestones |
|--------|------------|
| Q1 | Discovery, prioritization, architecture |
| Q2 | Build & pilot use cases |
| Q3 | Deployment, monitoring, scale |
| Q4 | Optimization & expansion |

---

This roadmap provides a repeatable structure for delivering AI programs with clarity, alignment, and measurable outcomes.
