# AI Adoption KPIs

Key performance indicators for measuring AI adoption and impact.

---

## Adoption KPIs
- % of users actively using AI features
- Frequency of AI-assisted tasks
- Reduction in manual work
- Time saved per workflow

---

## Operational KPIs
- Model uptime
- Response latency
- Integration reliability
- Error rates

---

## Business KPIs
- Cost savings
- Revenue impact
- Cycle time reduction
- Customer satisfaction improvements

---

## Model Performance KPIs
- Accuracy / precision / recall
- Drift detection frequency
- Data quality scores
- Retraining cadence

---

## Governance KPIs
- Policy compliance rate
- Risk review completion
- Responsible AI audit results

---

These KPIs provide a balanced view of adoption, performance, and business impact.
